## Hi there 👋

I'm a **Geospatial Engineer** building pipelines that turn raw satellite imagery and spatial data into **decision-ready insights**, from data acquisition and processing to machine learning and application delivery.

I enjoy taking complex geospatial tasks and turning them into **automated, reusable, reproducible solutions**. My work is Python-first, built on the geospatial ecosystem alongside Google Earth Engine, PyTorch, Scikit-learn, MLflow, and Optuna.

My focus areas are **agriculture, energy, and environmental monitoring**.

---

## 🔍 What I Work On

- **Satellite image processing:** large-area workflows in Google Earth Engine and Python (Rasterio, GDAL)
- **Geospatial data engineering:** automated, repeatable pipelines for ingesting, cleaning, and transforming spatial data
- **Machine learning for Earth observation:** classification and prediction with gradient boosting and deep learning
- **Time-series analysis:** vegetation and environmental indicators for monitoring change over time
- **Geospatial applications:** storing, serving, and visualizing results with PostGIS, GeoServer, and OpenLayers

---

## ⚙️ How I Work

- **Reproducible over one-off:** pipelines and scripts built to be rerun, reused, and maintained
- **Tracked experiments:** models versioned and compared with MLflow, tuned systematically with Optuna
- **Spatially aware validation:** spatial cross-validation to avoid the leakage that random splits cause in geospatial data
- **End-to-end thinking:** from raw data to a usable product, not just a notebook result
